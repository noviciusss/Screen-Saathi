# Screen Saathi — 2-3 Minute Video Demo Script

> **Video Duration:** 2 minutes 30 seconds  
> **Tools needed:** Chrome with Screen Saathi loaded, backend running, screen recording tool (OBS / Loom / Windows Game Bar Win+G).  
> **Language:** English narrative with Hindi voice queries to showcase multilingual capabilities.

---

### [0:00 – 0:30] Scene 1: The Problem & Introduction
* **Visual:** Your face (optional / corner bubble) + Chrome browser showing a dense, cluttered portal or Wikipedia article with complex layout.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > *"Hello everyone! Over 300 million people across India struggle with today's web — elderly parents, citizens who aren't fluent in English, and first-time internet users. Websites are packed with complex forms, dense text, and confusing buttons.*
  >
  > *To solve this for the Gnani.ai Great Indian AI Internship Challenge, I built **Screen Saathi** — an AI-powered voice companion for Chrome that explains any web page aloud in Indian languages, highlights buttons on-screen, and helps fill forms safely without ever acting behind your back."*

---

### [0:30 – 1:15] Scene 2: Live Page Understanding in Hindi (Gnani Prisma + Timbre)
* **Visual:** Open a Wikipedia article or news page (e.g. `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India`).
  1. Click the Screen Saathi extension icon to open the right side panel.
  2. Click **"इस पेज को तैयार करें"** (Prepare this page). Show the status update: *"पेज की जानकारी तैयार है"*.
  3. Press and hold the big **Talk** button.
* **Speaker says aloud into microphone (Hindi):**
  > *"यह पेज किस बारे में है और इसमें मुख्य जानकारी क्या है?"*
* **Visual Action:**
  * Release the button.
  * Point out the live transcript appearing under **"आपने कहा"**.
  * Point out the element outline highlighting the main heading on Wikipedia.
  * Listen to Screen Saathi's voice reply generated by **Gnani Timbre (Nalini voice)**:
    > *"मैं एक AI voice assistant हूँ। यह पेज भारत के बारे में है। यह भारत के इतिहास, संस्कृति और मुख्य तथ्यों की जानकारी देता है।"*
* **Speaker commentary:**
  > *"Notice how Screen Saathi streamed real-time Hindi audio using Gnani's Timbre TTS and highlighted the exact section on the webpage."*

---

### [1:15 – 2:05] Scene 3: Guided Form Filling with Safety & Confirmation Gate
* **Visual:** Switch tab to `fixtures/pages/form.html` (or a sample government service registration form).
  1. Click **"इस पेज को तैयार करें"**.
  2. Press and hold the **Talk** button.
* **Speaker says aloud into microphone:**
  > *"मुझे यह फॉर्म भरने में मदद करो।"*
* **Visual Action:**
  * Saathi responds in Hindi:
    > *"पहला फ़ील्ड 'पूरा नाम' है। अपना नाम बताएं।"*
  * User presses Talk:
    > *"राजेश कुमार"*
  * **Highlight:** A large confirmation card appears in the side panel with **"हाँ, भरें"** and **"नहीं"**.
  * User clicks **"हाँ, भरें"** (or says *"हाँ"*).
  * The input field on the webpage immediately populates with *"Rajesh Kumar"*.
* **Speaker commentary:**
  * Show the Submit button at the bottom of the form:
  > *"Here is the most critical safety invariant: Screen Saathi strictly refuses to click Submit, Pay, or Delete buttons. When all fields are done, Saathi reminds the user to review the details and click Submit themselves. Our AI assists — it never takes over."*

---

### [2:05 – 2:30] Scene 4: Security, PII Redaction & Conclusion
* **Visual:** Show the side panel privacy policy / scam detection badge.
* **Speaker commentary:**
  > *"Under the hood, Screen Saathi implements complete privacy-by-design:*
  > *1. Password and OTP fields are masked on-device as `[hidden]`.*
  > *2. Aadhaar, PAN, and phone numbers are automatically redacted before sending any data off-device.*
  > *3. Speech is powered by Gnani Prisma v4 for STT and Timbre v2.5 for TTS.*
  >
  > *Screen Saathi makes the open web accessible, safe, and voice-first for every Indian citizen. Thank you to the Gnani.ai team!"*

### Quick Recording Checklist Before You Hit Record:
1. `backend` is running in a terminal:
   ```powershell
   backend\.venv-win\Scripts\uvicorn backend.src.screen_saathi.app:app --reload --port 8000
   ```
2. Chrome extension is loaded and side panel opens smoothly.
3. Microphone is tested and browser volume is audible on your speakers/recording.
4. Have your tabs pre-opened:
   * Tab 1: `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India`
   * Tab 2: `d:\voice_gnani\fixtures\pages\form.html`
